Meet the team

Athletes who did this themselves.

Every advisor at Signing Day Advisors competed at the collegiate level and navigated the recruiting process firsthand.

Nareg Minassian

Yale University · Chemistry

Nareg graduated with distinction from Yale with a degree in Chemistry, while competing as a four-year NCAA Division I swimmer. He qualified for Olympic Trials, set multiple New England records, and ranks among Yale's all-time top ten in three events. Academically, Nareg was recognized as a CSC Academic All-District and a CSCAA Scholar All-American Honoree. He has held positions at AstraZeneca, Mass General Brigham, and the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, and he is currently pursuing a PhD in Chemistry.

James Dun Rappaport

Emory University · Finance

James graduated cum laude from Emory University in 2026 with a BBA in Finance and a minor in English. At Emory, he was an active peer tutor for both Statistics and English students and a founding member of the Goizueta Book Club. James has interned in biotechnology and impact investing, and is starting his career in M&A Advisory at FTI Consulting in the fall.

Ethan Ecsedy

Bowdoin College · Molecular & Cellular Biology

Ethan graduated cum laude from Bowdoin College in 2026, where he was a three-year starter on the varsity football team. He received a BS in Molecular and Cellular Biology and a minor in Economics and has leveraged these degrees across roles at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, Kura Therapeutics, and Convergent Therapeutics. He plans to pursue cancer biology PhD programs.

Finn Henry

Yale University · History

Finn graduated from Yale University in 2026, where he majored in History. At Yale, Finn was a member of the men's varsity swim team, where he competed as a distance swimmer and actively managed their alumni network. He has extensive experience in the political sphere, having served as both a policy and congressional intern. Finn is currently working in consulting in Washington, D.C.

Isabella "Izz"

Yale University · History of Science & Medicine

Isabella graduated from Yale University in 2026 with both a BA in the History of Science and Medicine and an MS in Personalized Medicine and Applied Engineering. While navigating this rigorous coursework, she swam for the women's varsity swim team, where she was a podium finisher at several meets. Isabella has done extensive medical research at Yale and the University of Pennsylvania, and is currently working in the 3D Design & Innovation Lab at Northwell Health.
