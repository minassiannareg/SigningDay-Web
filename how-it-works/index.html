Standardize → Benchmark

Every number on the same scale. Measured against the right target.

Raw stats aren't comparable on their own — so we convert, then compare against each school's real data.

Standardization

A GPA, a test score, and a swim time don't compare directly. Every input is converted onto one common scale before anything is combined.

3.95 GPA  ·  1480 SAT  ·  52.10 free  →  one scale

University benchmark

Each school's target isn't one cutoff. We build a picture of that school's own enrolled class from its own published data, and compare against that — not a universal bar.

What each read means.

Every school gives one of three reads. Coaches call these the A, B, and C bands.

Long shotFits with a pushStrong fit
Band A

Strong fit

At or above the school's usual students. In range on academics alone.

Band B

Fits with a push

A little below. A coach spending a spot can close the gap.

Band C

Long shot

Well below. Only a standout swimmer earns one of the few spots.

A structured read to plan with, not a guarantee — an admissions committee weighs far more than any framework can capture.

Limitations

What the Compass does not mean.

Not a prediction

Admissions are holistic and dynamic — no index can fully capture them.

Not a guarantee

We don't guarantee admission, offers, roster spots, or scholarships.
