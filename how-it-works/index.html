What each fit means.

Every school gives one of three reads. Coaches call these the A, B, and C bands.

Long shotFits with a pushStrong fit

Strong fit

At or above the school's usual students. In range on academics alone.

Fits with a push

A little below. A coach spending a spot can close the gap.

Long shot

Well below. Only a standout swimmer earns one of the few spots.

These are a guide to plan with. A coach's review makes the real call.
